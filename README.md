<details>
<summary><b>📋 Copyit features</b></summary>

- Save, search, tag and sort clips into folders. Filters: All, Links, Code, Multimedia
- Preview button opens a small viewer window
- **Scan to open:** offline QR codes for short clips (text or links up to 5 KB)
- Import and export: JSON, CSV, TXT, Markdown
- Two themes (Multicolor, Black & White), Light / Dark / Auto
- Shortcut: `Ctrl+Shift+Y` (`Cmd+Shift+Y` on Mac)
- Clips live in Chrome storage. Nothing is uploaded

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>🗂 Archiv AI features</b></summary>

- One-tab Inbox: preview, save to vault, export or discard
- Duplicate-aware saving, topic tags (up to four per chat)
- Nine formats: PDF, Word, image, Markdown, HTML, JSON, text, Notion, Obsidian
- Redaction of keys, tokens, emails and phones
- Searchable library with folders, tags, backup and activity dashboard
- 239-tool directory, 15 hand-written adapters, auto-detect and Teach Archiv AI
- Works in Chrome, Edge and Brave. Shortcut: `Alt+Shift+E`

</details>
